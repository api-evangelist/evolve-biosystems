# Infinant Health

> Infinant Health (formerly Evolve Biosystems) is a privately held life-sciences company
> in Sacramento, California, spun out of the Foods for Health Institute at the University
> of California, Davis. It commercializes B. infantis EVC001 — a strain of Bifidobacterium
> longum subsp. infantis that metabolizes human milk oligosaccharides — as the consumer
> infant probiotic Evivo, and is developing INF108 as a drug candidate aimed at reducing
> necrotizing enterocolitis in premature infants.
